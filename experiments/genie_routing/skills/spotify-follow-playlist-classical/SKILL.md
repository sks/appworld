# Follow classical artists from my playlists

Any classical-genre song sitting in your playlists has artists you should follow.

Sign into Spotify. Walk every playlist. For songs marked classical (or equivalent genre), collect their artists and follow each one you are not already following. Skip non-classical tracks. Finish as an action-only success (no answer string).

## Tools you will need

Spotify login, playlist/song genre inspection, follow artist; supervisor complete_task.
