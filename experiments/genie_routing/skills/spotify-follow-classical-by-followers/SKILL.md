# Follow classical artists with enough followers

Follow every classical artist on Spotify who has at least twenty-two followers.

Sign into Spotify. Discover classical artists (search or browse as the APIs allow). For each classical artist whose follower count is at least 22, follow them if you are not already following. Skip artists below the threshold and non-classical artists.

Finish as an action-only success (no answer string).

## Tools you will need

Spotify login, artist search/show, follow artist; supervisor complete_task.
