# Movie recommendations by text

Someone named Christopher has texted asking for movie recommendations. Your job is to answer that text once, with a short comma-separated list of titles that actually match what he asked for — not a dump of every movie you keep in notes.

Start by signing into Phone and Simple Note the usual way: look up your profile and account passwords in the vault, then log into each app. On Phone, find Christopher’s recent messages and **read his inbound text first**. That message is the filter — often a director’s name or a similar constraint. Do not guess the filter from memory or from the note’s full contents.

Next open Simple Note and find the movie list note. Read it carefully. Each entry usually carries attributes such as a director line. Keep only the titles whose attributes satisfy Christopher’s request. If he asked for one director, leave every other film out, even if they are excellent.

Send **one** text back to the phone number from his thread, titles separated by commas, filtered set only. Then mark the errand complete as an action-only task (no answer string). Do not send a second message “to be safe,” do not spawn a follow-up whose job is to “send everything / don’t filter,” and do not paste the whole note into SMS.

## Tools you will need

Phone search/show/send text; Simple Note search/show note; supervisor profile, passwords, and complete_task.
